Why "The Local Minimum"?

In mathematics, a local minimum is the best solution that is nearby, which is not always the best solution there is. Surgeons know this: the robotic or endovascular approach you keep struggling to optimize, when you should step back and switch to open.

In the OR, "local" means a small, targeted dose of injected anesthetic, usually used in short cases. That is the idea here: a brief, roughly weekly update on AI and how it relates to surgery and medicine.
