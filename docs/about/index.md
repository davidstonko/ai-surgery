I'm a vascular surgeon and applied mathematician. I work across the AI stack, from the mathematics of learning to deployment at the bedside, and I have built clinical machine learning models since 2011.

At Johns Hopkins I'm a clinical fellow in Vascular Surgery and Endovascular Therapy. I advise the Department of Surgery on AI strategy, I'm a member of the [Data Science and AI Institute](https://ai.jhu.edu/), and I sit on the Johns Hopkins Medicine workgroup deploying OpenEvidence. I founded the department's AI and Surgery Interest Group and write its weekly newsletter, [The Local Minimum](../). I also build open-source Claude tools now used across the department; they work from placeholders, so no patient information reaches the model.

At Meta I work as a Medical Knowledge Expert on frontier medical AI reasoning, currently focused on long time horizon reinforcement learning tasks in medicine and surgery: building rubrics, testing frontier models and studying where they fail. At [Catalio Capital Management](https://www.cataliocapital.com/team/david-stonko) I lead investment diligence on health care companies, now mostly in AI.

My research is building the mathematical foundations for world models of vascular and endovascular surgery: [anatomy-informed neural networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21332), which build anatomic knowledge into deep learning, and an SE(3) formulation of how a stiff guidewire deforms the aortoiliac tree ([I'm looking for collaborators](../collaborate/)). Earlier, I built neural networks that predicted pediatric traumatic brain injury outcomes better than CT grading systems, forecast trauma center volume and acuity, and assigned trauma activation levels for Vanderbilt's Life Flight team. More is on the [Research](../research/) and [MedTech](../medtech/) pages.

I have published more than 130 research articles, reviews and book chapters, and six clinical practice guidelines cite my work, including the 2025 European Society for Vascular Surgery vascular trauma guidelines and the 2025 Society for Vascular Surgery claudication update.

## Training

- Clinical Fellow, Vascular Surgery and Endovascular Therapy, The Johns Hopkins Hospital (2025 to present)

- Board certified, American Board of Surgery (General Surgery)
- MD, Vanderbilt University School of Medicine
- MS, Applied Mathematics, and BS, Mathematics and Biological Sciences, University of Maryland, Baltimore County
- Alpha Omega Alpha

The views on this site and in The Local Minimum are my own and do not represent Johns Hopkins University, Johns Hopkins Medicine or Meta.
