## My device: a pressurized IV fluid bag system

In trauma and sepsis, how quickly a patient gets fluid or blood can matter. Caring for these patients, I saw an unfilled need for a simpler way to deliver fluid and blood faster at the bedside. So I built a prototype and filed a patent for a device that keeps a hanging fluid bag under pressure as it empties, with the goal of improving care for surgical patients when time is short.

## Consulting and device development

I have worked with device and digital health companies at every stage, from early prototypes to FDA submissions. Some examples:

- Machine learning strategy and product development for aortic and carotid imaging algorithms.
- A large animal safety and efficacy trial of an intranasal brain-cooling device for traumatic brain injury, whose data supported its FDA IDE application.
- Designing the IDE-enabling large animal trial for an autotransfusion device and supporting its FDA interactions.
- Cadaver testing of an ultrasound-detectable implantable marker to collect data for FDA review.
- Large animal studies and STTR preparation for a new class of devices to treat extremity ischemia.
- Co-developing a vascular isolation and perfusion device through institutional technology transfer.
