If you have never used AI for anything academic, start here. [Dr. Marissa Famularo](https://www.marissafamularo.com), a vascular surgeon who builds practical AI tools and teaches AI literacy to physicians, has shared a [free literature digest prompt](https://statup.app/briefing.html) that turns Claude into a daily reading list for your specialty.

You paste one setup prompt, answer a few questions about your field, the journals you follow and the kinds of studies you care about, and Claude builds the rest. Each morning it searches PubMed, drops papers you have already seen, applies the quality rules you gave it, and puts three to eight papers on one page you can read on your phone.

## My thoughts

This is a low-stakes way to see what these tools can do. It never touches patient information, and the worst case is a paper you did not need to read. Setup takes about ten minutes. It needs the Claude desktop app, and the daily automatic version needs a paid Claude plan. Her site has more, including a free AI course for clinicians. I talked with her and she was glad to have it shared here.
